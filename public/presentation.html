Core Action

Creating a task

Two ways to create a task: Quick Add from the header, or the full Task Modal. Both are available from anywhere in the app.

⚡ Method 1: Quick Add (3 seconds)

  1. Press Ctrl+N or click the "Quick add..." field in the header bar
  2. Type the task title (e.g. "Review influencer posts - KSA")
  3. Press Enter
✔ Task is created with default values: Medium priority, Backlog status, Morning shift, your name as owner, your office/country/team.
✔ A success toast appears at the top of the screen.

📝 Method 2: Full Task Modal

  1. Click the "+ New" button in the header bar
  2. The Task Modal opens with all available fields
  3. Fill in: Title (required), Details, Priority (Low/Medium/High), Status (Backlog/In Progress/Waiting/Blocked/Done), Owner, Office, Country, Team, Shift, Due date
  4. Optionally add a Definition of Done (DoD) checklist
  5. Click Save or press Enter
Tip: Use the modal for tasks that need full details from the start.

Task fields explained

Each task in Hub Manager has these fields. Understanding them helps you and your team stay aligned.

Title

The task name. Keep it concise and action-oriented (e.g. "Review influencer brief - KSA"). This is the only required field.

Details

Optional description. Add context, links, or instructions for whoever picks up the task.

Priority

High — urgent, needs attention now. Medium — normal priority. Low — nice-to-have, no deadline pressure.

Status

Backlog — not started. In Progress — actively working. Waiting — awaiting external input. Blocked — cannot proceed. Done — completed.

Owner

The person responsible. Defaults to you when you create the task. Can be reassigned to anyone on your team.

Office, Country, Team

Which operating hub this task belongs to. Used for filtering and regional reporting. Auto-filled from your profile.

Shift

Morning, Mid, or Night. Helps track which shift a task belongs to, especially during handovers.

Due date

Deadline for completion. Tasks past due are flagged as "overdue" in filters and the Command Center.

Carry-over

Mark a task as "carry-over" if it needs to continue into the next shift. These automatically surface in handovers.

Core Action

Editing & updating tasks

✏️ Method 1: Click to edit

  1. Navigate to Task Register (sidebar or Alt+3)
  2. Click on any task to open the Task Modal with all fields pre-filled
  3. Modify any field: title, details, priority, status, owner, etc.
  4. Click Save to apply changes
Changes are saved immediately and reflected in all views.

🗑️ Deleting a task

  1. Open the task in the Task Modal
  2. Click the Trash/Delete icon
  3. Confirm deletion — the task is removed from all views
Note: Only available if your role has "task.delete" permission.

📋 Method 2: Kanban board drag-and-drop

  1. Switch Task Register to Kanban view (toggle in toolbar)
  2. Tasks are displayed in columns by status: Backlog, In Progress, Waiting, Blocked, Done
  3. Drag a task card from one column to another to change its status
  4. The status updates instantly — no modal needed

Power Feature

Filtering & searching tasks

🔍 Quick Search (Ctrl+K)

  1. Press Ctrl+K from anywhere in the app
  2. The search field in the header bar is focused
  3. Type any keyword — matches against task title, owner, office, country, and team
  4. Results appear inline as you type

🎯 Preset filters

  1. In the Task Register, click the filter dropdowns above the task list
  2. Filter by: Status, Team, Country, Shift
  3. Click column headers to sort ascending/descending
  4. Type in the search box for free-text search
Shortcut filters from Command Center: click "Action Risks" to auto-filter all high-risk tasks. Click "Carry-over" to see multi-shift tasks.

🧹 Bulk actions

  1. In Table view, click the checkbox next to each task to select
  2. Or click the header checkbox to select all visible tasks
  3. Use the bulk action bar to: change status, change priority, reassign owner, or delete
Only available if your role has "task.bulk" permission.

Shift Handover overview

The handover system is how outgoing teams pass context, tasks, and watchouts to the incoming shift. No information is lost.

📋 What a handover contains

Outgoing/Incoming — who is handing off and who is receiving. Shifts — e.g. Morning to Mid. Tasks — selected tasks being handed over (auto-selected carry-overs + manual picks). Watchouts — free-text notes about blockers, escalations, or anything the next shift needs to know. Status — Pending (awaiting acknowledgment) or Acknowledged.

🤝 Acknowledgment flow

When you submit a handover, the incoming team member sees it in their Shift Handover view. They must click Acknowledge to confirm receipt. Both parties can see the acknowledgment status. This creates an audit trail.

Creating a handover (step by step)

📲 The handover wizard

  1. Go to Shift Handover in the sidebar (or Alt+4)
  2. Click "+ New Handover" to start the wizard
  3. Step 1 - Template: Choose a template (e.g. "Morning → Mid Standard"). This pre-fills shift info and default watchouts. You can also start from scratch.
  4. Step 2 - Details: Select your team, outgoing/incoming shifts, outgoing person (you), and incoming person (select from team members list). Choose offices.
  5. Step 3 - Tasks: Select which tasks to hand over. Carry-over and high-priority tasks are auto-selected. You can add or remove any task.
  6. Step 4 - Watchouts: Write any critical notes for the incoming shift. Things like "Campaign review pending client approval" or "Influencer brief blocked".
  7. Step 5 - Submit: Review everything and click Submit. The handover appears in both outgoing and incoming views.

Selecting tasks for handover

📋 Step 3: Task selection

  1. In the handover wizard, step 3 shows a list of all tasks in your team/office
  2. Tasks marked as carry-over and high-priority are auto-selected (pre-checked)
  3. You can click to add or remove any task
  4. The "Auto-select" button re-applies the smart selection if you've changed things
  5. Each task shows: title, status badge, priority, owner, due date
Tip: Only hand over tasks that are still relevant to the next shift. Completed tasks (Done) don't need to be handed over.

Watchouts, submit & acknowledge

⚠️ Step 4: Writing watchouts

  1. In the watchouts field, write any critical information for the incoming shift
  2. Be specific: "Campaign review blocked — awaiting client approval on v3" is better than "Campaign not done"
  3. Number your items for clarity (the template may pre-fill defaults)
  4. Mention escalation contacts if applicable

📤 Step 5: Submit & acknowledge

  1. Review all details on the final step
  2. Click Submit — the handover is created with status "Pending"
  3. The incoming team member will see it in their handover list
  4. They click Acknowledge to confirm receipt
  5. Status changes to "Acknowledged" — visible to both parties

Pro Feature

Handover templates

Templates save time by pre-filling shift details, default watchouts, and task selection logic. They ensure consistency across every handover.

Morning → Mid Standard

Default template for the most common transition. Pre-fills: Morning as outgoing, Mid as incoming, Operations Team. Default watchouts ask you to review carry-over items, check SLA compliance, and verify escalation paths.

Mid → Night Handover

Specialized for the critical mid-to-night transition. Pre-fills: APAC client focus, coverage reports, influencer posting schedules, emergency contact availability.

How templates work

When you select a template, it auto-sets: team, from/to shifts, and default watchout text. It also auto-selects eligible tasks (carry-over + high priority). You can modify everything after template selection.

AI Feature

Using the AI Copilot

The AI Copilot is your assistant for summarizing tasks, flagging risks, and generating briefs. It reads your actual task board data.

🤖 How to use it

  1. Go to AI Workspace in the sidebar
  2. The conversation starts with an automated status message
  3. Type your question in natural language, like:
    "What's the status of today's high-priority tasks?"
  4. The AI responds with a concise, structured answer based on your data

💭 Example prompts

  1. "Summarize all tasks in my team that are blocked"
  2. "Which tasks are overdue today?"
  3. "Generate a handover brief for Morning to Mid shift"
  4. "What are the risks I should escalate before my shift ends?"
  5. "Show me all tasks assigned to Sara"
